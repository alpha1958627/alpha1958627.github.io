This project studies the dynamics of droplet migration driven by curvature gradients. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for applications in droplet-based microfluidics, oil recovery, and surface coating technologies.

## Research Focus

- **Migration Mechanisms**: How curvature gradients induce droplet motion
- **Velocity Profiles**: Quantitative analysis of migration velocities
- **Effect of Fluctuations**: Role of thermal noise in migration dynamics
