The stochastic lubrication equation (SLE) provides a theoretical framework for describing the dynamics of thin films and interfaces in the presence of thermal fluctuations. This research focuses on developing and validating numerical methods for solving the SLE.

## Methodology

- **Numerical Schemes**: Finite difference methods for SLE discretization
- **Parallel Computing**: GPU-accelerated simulations using CUDA
- **Validation**: Comparison with analytical solutions and experimental data
