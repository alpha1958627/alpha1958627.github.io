I am a first-year master's student majoring in Mechanics at USTC. In 2023, I joined the [Surface and Interface Physics of Fluids (SIPF) Laboratory](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~tsi/) .  Under the supervision of Professor [Ting Si](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~tsi/) and Researcher [Chengxi Zhao](https://faculty.ustc.edu.cn/zhaochengxi/zh_CN/index.htm), my research focuses on thin **film flows** under the influence of **thermal fluctuations**, fluid stability, and **stochastic lubrication equations**.
